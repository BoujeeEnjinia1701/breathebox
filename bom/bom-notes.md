# BOM notes

- Prices are indicative USD for single prototype quantities in September 2026, checked against no specific supplier yet. They are estimates for concept review.
- Rows 1 to 12 match the numbered callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Row 13 (seals, fasteners and wiring) is not drawn.
- Total parts cost is about $243, about $23 (10 %) over the $220 prototype budget in `project.yaml`. A budget change is proposed and awaiting Amish (see `docs/REVIEW.md`).
- The counterflow core (row 2) is the largest single cost and sets the heat recovery figure. A home-made crossflow core from corrugated plastic sheet would cost about $10 but recover only about 50 to 65 % (estimate).
- The unit runs from a certified 24 V plug-in adapter. There is no mains wiring inside the unit.
