# BOM notes

- Prices are indicative USD for single prototype quantities in September 2026. Every line is priced, with a supplier or supplier type; no price has been confirmed with a named supplier. They are estimates for TRL 3 review.
- Rows 1 to 12 match the numbered callouts in `media/exploded.png` and the parts in `cad/src/model.py`. Row 13 (seals, fasteners, wiring and the input fuse) is not in the exploded view; the bolts and the cable gland are in the model.
- Total parts cost is **$283.00** (BBX-CAL-001 section 9, computed by `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py`), $28 over the $255 budget in `project.yaml` approved by Amish on 2026-09-26 (BBX-DDR-002). **R15 is not met.** The rise comes from parts the concept needed but did not list, added when the design was made constructable on 2026-09-30 (BBX-DDR-003): partitions, filter seats, grilles and latches in row 1 (+$18), hood back plates and collar flanges in row 10 (+$2), bracket rails, cleats and foot in row 11 (+$4), and bolts, gland and anti-slip tape in row 13 (+$4). A budget of $283 is proposed, awaiting Amish (design decisions register, BBX-DEC-001).
- Changes from TRL 2 ($243) to the concept at TRL 3 ($255): larger blowers in rows 3 and 4 (+$8, because the TRL 2 fans could not reach 70 m³/h), collars through the panel in row 10 (+$2), and a 2 A fuse and holder in row 13 (+$2).
- The counterflow core (row 2) is the largest single cost and sets the heat recovery figure. A home-made crossflow core from corrugated plastic sheet would cost about $10 but recover only about 50 to 65 % (estimate).
- The unit runs from a certified 24 V plug-in adapter. There is no mains wiring inside the unit.
- Decided by Amish on 2026-10-02 (BBX-DEC-001): a bought no-drill sash jammer or adjustable security bar, wedged in the inner track above the raised sash, is part of the kit (about $10; R12). It is not yet a line in `bom.csv` or in the $283.00 total; with it the parts cost is about $293. The first prototype's wall foot is at 400 mm, so the struts in row 11 are longer than the lengths now listed; row 11 is to be re-specified with the model.
