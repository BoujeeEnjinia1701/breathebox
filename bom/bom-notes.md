# BOM notes

- Prices are indicative USD for single prototype quantities in September 2026. Every line is priced, with a supplier or supplier type; no price has been confirmed with a named supplier. They are estimates for TRL 3 review.
- Rows 1 to 12 and row 14 match the numbered callouts in `media/exploded.png` and the parts in `cad/src/model.py`. Row 13 (seals, fasteners, wiring and the input fuse) is not in the exploded view; the bolts and the cable gland are in the model.
- Value-engineering target: USD 255. Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 294 (USD 39 over the target). The total of **$294.00** over 14 lines is computed by `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` (BBX-CAL-001 section 9); the target is `budget_usd` in `project.yaml`, set by Amish on 2026-09-26 (BBX-DDR-002), and is unchanged. R15 is over the value-engineering target by $39. The rise from the concept's $255 comes from parts the concept needed but did not list, added when the design was made constructable on 2026-09-30 (BBX-DDR-003): partitions, filter seats, grilles and latches in row 1 (+$18), hood back plates and collar flanges in row 10 (+$2), bracket rails, cleats and foot in row 11 (+$4), and bolts, gland and anti-slip tape in row 13 (+$4); and from the 2026-10-02 decisions: the sash jammer in row 14 (+$10) and longer struts in row 11 (+$1). The savings worth trying are in the design decisions register (BBX-DEC-001).
- Changes from TRL 2 ($243) to the concept at TRL 3 ($255): larger blowers in rows 3 and 4 (+$8, because the TRL 2 fans could not reach 70 m³/h), collars through the panel in row 10 (+$2), and a 2 A fuse and holder in row 13 (+$2).
- The counterflow core (row 2) is the largest single cost and sets the heat recovery figure. A home-made crossflow core from corrugated plastic sheet would cost about $10 but recover only about 50 to 65 % (estimate).
- The unit runs from a certified 24 V plug-in adapter. There is no mains wiring inside the unit.
- Decided by Amish on 2026-10-02 (BBX-DEC-001): a bought no-drill sash jammer or adjustable security bar, wedged in the inner track above the raised sash, is part of the kit (R12). It is row 14, $10.00, the middle of a typical retail range of about $8 to $12; its mass is estimated at 0.30 kg.
- Decided by Amish on 2026-10-02 (BBX-DEC-001): the first prototype's wall foot is at 400 mm above the floor (was 500 mm). Row 11 is re-specified: each strut is cut 621 mm long (was 543 mm), 595 mm between end holes, rising at 55°. The extra 0.16 m of 20 x 1.5 mm aluminium tube at about $6 per metre raises row 11 from $16.00 to $17.00. Bracket mass rises from 1.23 to 1.27 kg.
